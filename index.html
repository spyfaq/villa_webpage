Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

The practical details guests ask us most often. If your question isn’t here, message us and we’ll answer it.

Where is Olga’s Luxury Villa located?

The villa is in Kontrakia, on a hillside about 6 km from Corfu Town, with panoramic views over the Ionian Sea, Gouvia Marina and Corfu Town itself. Corfu Airport is roughly 15 minutes away by car.

How many guests can stay at the villa?

The villa sleeps up to 6 guests across 3 bedrooms.

How many bedrooms and bathrooms does the villa have?

There are 3 bedrooms and 2.5 bathrooms — two full bathrooms and a ground-floor guest WC.

Does the villa have a private pool?

Yes. The villa has its own private infinity pool with panoramic sea views, for the exclusive use of guests staying at the villa. There are sun loungers on the pool terrace.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 15:00 and check-out is by 11:00. If you are arriving on a late flight, message us in advance and we will do our best to accommodate you.

Is there air conditioning?

Yes, there is air conditioning throughout the villa.

Is there Wi-Fi at the villa?

Yes, high-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the villa, and there is a desk area if you need to work during your stay.

Is there parking at the villa?

Yes, the villa has private parking on site at no extra cost.

Are pets allowed?

No, we are not able to accept pets at the villa.

Is there a kitchen?

Yes, the villa has a fully equipped kitchen with an indoor dining area, plus an outdoor BBQ and dining area on the terrace.

How far is the nearest beach?

Kontokali Beach is about 5 minutes away by car, and Dassia Beach — known for clear water and water sports — is about 10 minutes. Gouvia Marina, with its waterfront restaurants and cafés, is also about 5 minutes away.

How far is Corfu Old Town?

Corfu Old Town, a UNESCO World Heritage site, is about 10 minutes away by car — roughly 6 km from the villa.

Can I book directly with the owner?

Yes. You can send an enquiry through the form on this site or message us on WhatsApp, and we will confirm availability personally — usually within 12 hours. Booking direct means no platform service fee, which typically saves 15% or more. The villa is also listed on Airbnb and Booking.com if you prefer to book there.
